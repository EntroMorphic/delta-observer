# Arithmetic access: cumulative finding and decision

## Established before further diagnostics

The retention-transfer v2 candidate is rejected, not promoted. Its complete
results, seeds, gates, examples and controls are in
`native_retention_transfer_results.md`. In the fresh locked comparison:

- Untouched arithmetic confirmed content: **39/40**; native exact: **0/40**.
- All twelve adaptations: **5–10/40** arithmetic content, despite bare-integer
  responses and improved overall exact totals.
- Untouched both-operation content: **19/20** pairs; every adapter: **1/20**.
- Projection fails fresh retention in all seeds and loses ordinary arithmetic
  exact successes in seeds 0 and 2. Four protected continuation gradients are
  not a broad retention guarantee. Replay and random controls are preserved.
- Many adapted outputs equal the unchanged product: **10–20/40**, depending
  on arm/seed. This is an output observation, not proof of an internal circuit.

The earlier local schema-damage separability result remains established, but
does not resolve the larger native-arithmetic regression. No best seed, scoring
change, or suppressed failure is used to claim improvement.

## Mechanism alternatives still open

1. **Access/output-path change:** useful computation remains available but the
   adapted greedy answer path selects an early number or an unhelpful shortcut.
2. **Prompt-conditioned computation damage:** adapted execution no longer
   constructs the relevant result on these inputs, even under reasonable
   alternative output requests.
3. **Mixed mechanisms:** prompt binding, numeric selection and available serial
   computation can all change. The alternatives need not be exclusive.

Native success proves capability on these specific prompts, not an architecture-
wide capacity guarantee. Failed alternative prompting would not prove weights
have irreversibly lost a capability. Successful reasoning requests would show
recoverability under that intervention, not that reasoning text is necessary
or that a deployable exact-answer remedy has been found. Prior failed worked-
replay training remains negative evidence against an untested universal fix.

## Decision

No additional training yet. Run fixed inference-only output/access controls on
the exposed 40 arithmetic cases, using untouched and all three ordinary seeds.
Do not select examples or a best seed. Do not supply native workings, correct
answer choices, or calculated prefixes. Keep original score artifacts immutable.
The protocol is `native_arithmetic_access_protocol.md`; all new results are
post-opening diagnostics, not fresh confirmation.

## Completed access controls

`native_arithmetic_access_results.md` records the completed inference experiment.
The reasoning request yields untouched 40/40 and ordinary seeds 15/40, 5/40,
34/40 confirmed content, versus original ordinary 6/40, 7/40, 10/40. All seeds
lose some previously confirmed answers. Prefix continuations also recover some
cases but are not native exact compliance. A one-token numeric decoder gives
untouched 0/40 while leaving ordinary counts unchanged. Adapter-off generations
match native tokens exactly on all 120 ordinary-seed/case controls.

These observations establish partial conditional recoverability without further
training, not reliable remediation or a unique root mechanism. They reject
complete universal capability erasure as the explanation of every adapted
failure, but do not distinguish internal computation from answer selection on
every input. No candidate is approved. A same-history causal fork with no
answer-bearing prefix is the next proposed localization step.

## Completed same-history causal forks

`native_same_history_results.md` now records that localization step. With active
adapters and native nonnumeric reasoning prefixes, content rises 15→38, 5→36,
34→39/40; seed 2 loses a previously correct 71 answer and instead computes 512.
On identical empty original histories, off/on first-token top-1 differs in
40/40 cases for every seed, so previous differing generated tokens cannot be
the only adapter effect. Conversely, adapted boxed-answer prefixes reduce
native adapter-off reasoning content from 40/40 to 15/40, 5/40, 34/40.
The native failures there are parseable wrong answers, not only formatting.

All 720 paired histories have identical inputs; 960 registered self-replay
checks match donor continuations. Aliases are explicit. This supports early
output-history routing as a substantial causal mediator on these exposed cases,
not a unique root cause, fixed-prefix transfer guarantee, or bare-answer fix.
No additional training or candidate promotion occurred.
